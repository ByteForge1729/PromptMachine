# Brainstorm

Load only when the user says yes to the offer in Phase 0. The point is to widen the idea
before the questions narrow it. Nothing here is a decision.

## Rules

- **Wide, not safe.** At least eight directions in the first pass. Two or three sensible
  options is the failure this mode exists to prevent.
- **One per angle.** Take each direction from a different angle in the table below, so
  they cannot be eight versions of the same idea.
- **Include the odd ones.** At least two directions should feel slightly wrong. Say which,
  so the user knows you know.
- **No recommendation, no ranking, no "which one?".** Ask what pulls them and what puts
  them off.
- **Short.** A name and two plain sentences each: what it is, and what it would be like
  to use. No feature lists, no technology.
- **Their ideas outrank yours.** When they add a direction, build on it before offering
  more of your own.
- **Nothing is logged as a decision.** Not even enthusiasm.

## Angles

| Angle | The question behind it |
|---|---|
| A different person | Who else could this be for, and what would they need it to be? |
| One thing, taken too far | What if the single best part were the whole product? |
| The opposite mood | If the obvious version is serious, what is the playful one? And the reverse |
| A different shape | The same idea as a game, a poster, a daily message, a physical object, a tool, a toy |
| Less | What is left if it does one thing and has one screen? |
| Together | What changes if two people use it at once, or one person makes it for another? |
| Time | What if it lasts one minute, or grows over a year? |
| Borrowed | How would a museum, a board game, a street market or a kitchen solve this? |
| The wrong one | The version that sounds like a mistake but is interesting |
| What it refuses | The version defined by something it will never do |

## Flow

1. One line: *"Here are some directions. Nothing is decided. Tell me what pulls you and
   what puts you off."*
2. First pass: 8 to 12 directions.
3. They react in their own words. Do not use the question tool here; a list of options
   would narrow it.
4. Second pass, if they want one: 4 to 6 more, pushed further toward whatever pulled
   them, plus one that crosses two of their favourites. Stop after the second pass unless
   they ask for more.
5. Synthesis.

## Synthesis

Five lines at most, in their words where possible:

- **Pulled toward:** ...
- **Pushed away from:** ...
- **Worth keeping from the odd ones:** ...
- **Still open:** ...
- **The idea now, in one sentence:** ...

Ask "Is that right?" once. Then:

- rewrite the Phase 0 read-back from it;
- save it in `state.json` under `brainstorm` and in `00-brief.md`;
- carry "pushed away from" into E21 (what it must not be mistaken for) and "still open"
  into the first rounds;
- treat nothing in it as answered. A direction they liked still gets its questions.
