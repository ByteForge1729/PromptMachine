## Non-goals

- **Holding, moving or verifying money.** Regulated territory; UPI apps already do it. (D1)
- **Personal budgeting or spending analytics.** A different product. (A8)
- **Receipt scanning.** Heavy to build, low value for equal splits. (A8)
- **A web app.** Everyone involved has an Android phone. (A8)
- **Exchange rates.** One currency per group removes them. (D3)

## Success

Three real friend groups, about 15 people, still adding expenses in week 8 after
release to the internal testing track. (A9)
