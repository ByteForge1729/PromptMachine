# Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Damage | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A misread question (lost sign, wrong digit) is practised as real | high | Students learn the wrong thing | Scan beside every question, misread reports, 2 reports hide (D21, A8) | mitigated |
| Topic map read as a prediction; a student skips a topic that is asked | medium | Real exam harm | Fixed wording, never greyed out, "not asked yet" branch (D20, A18) | mitigated |
| AI merges two different papers, or misses a duplicate | medium | Map wrong | "Merged by AI" label and undo (A27) | mitigated |
| Questions built from IITB papers circulate outside the institute | high | Institute objection | Share pages not indexed | **accepted by user** (D11) |
| Launching empty: nobody uploads to an empty site | high | Cold start fails | Upload prompts on empty courses | **accepted by user** (D22) |
| Sending institute exam papers to an outside AI provider breaks a policy | unknown | Takedown request | Check department AI policies (see 09) | open, `[UNVERIFIED]` |
| No sponsor appears; v1 never starts | medium | AI features never ship | v0 is complete without them | accepted |
| Sponsor logo makes the site "commercial" under free hosting terms | low | Forced plan change | Check terms before adding any logo | open |
| Bursty exam-week traffic hits free-tier limits | medium | Slow or failing uploads | Queue, caps, alerts (A2, A6, A22) | mitigated |
