# Scope

## v0: a trustworthy past-paper bank

| Feature | Why it is in v0 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts, synced across phone and laptop | One place for everything | D28, A9, A25 |
| Upload by PDF or phone camera | Seniors fill the bank | B4, D3, D28, A19, A29 |
| Reading papers: questions, figures, handwritten solutions, scan snippets | The core data | B4, D10, D21, D25, A8, A30 |
| Duplicate detection | Keeps the map honest | D24, A27 |
| Syllabus and topics, with versions | Topics come from the syllabus | D7, D23, D26, A26 |
| Topic map | The first screen | D9, D14, D20, D27, A11, A12 |
| Practice by topic with real past questions | The simple version is most of the value | D16 |
| Exam-week plan | In the brief | D19, A28 |
| Share links for question sets and plans | Anyone, no account | D11, A16, A35 |

## v1: AI practice questions

Starts when a sponsor exists (D13) **and** the checker catches at least 9 of 10 planted
wrong solutions (D12).

| Feature | Source |
|---|---|
| Shared AI-generated questions per course and topic, generated once, batch-priced | D1 |
| Cross-check (several independent solutions plus a code check in a sandbox) | D2, D12, A1 |
| Red card for unconfirmed solutions; never in plans | D2 |
| Personal generation with the student's own API key; private, shareable by the owner | D1, D5, A5 |

## Non-goals

- **Current-semester papers, quizzes or assignments.** Academic integrity. (D3)
- **Predicting exam questions.** The map describes the past only. (A17)
- **A leaderboard.** Removed by you. (D6)
- **A doubt-solving chat.** Different product. (A17)
- **Other colleges.** IITB courses only in v0. (A17)

## Success

By the end of this semester's end-sems: at least 5 courses with 5 or more papers each, and
at least 200 students who practised 10 or more questions. (D15)
