# Users and flows

## Who

Rohan, three weeks before the MA 109 end-sem, on his laptop in the library, wanting to know
where to spend his time. Arjun, a senior, with a folder of old papers on his phone.

## Flows

### F-1 Arjun uploads a paper from his phone
1. Signs in with his @iitb.ac.in email (login link). (A9)
2. Taps Upload → Camera → photographs 6 pages; blurry pages get "Retake this page". (A19)
3. Picks course MA 109, year 2023, exam type End-sem. (D24)
   - Year and exam match the current semester: refused, "Only papers from past semesters."
     (D3)
4. Upload finishes; processing continues in the background, page by page. (A2)
5. If the AI finds it is a duplicate of an existing paper, it is merged and marked "merged
   by AI", with undo for Arjun and anyone viewing. (D24, A27)

### F-2 First paper for a new course
1. No syllabus exists: Arjun is asked to paste the syllabus from the course catalogue.
2. A second student confirms it before topics are published. (D7)
3. Until then, questions are listed by paper, not by topic.

### F-3 Rohan opens MA 109 on his laptop
1. Sees the topic map: every current-syllabus topic, shaded by weighted frequency, with
   "based on 8 papers" and the line "How often a topic came up before. Any syllabus topic
   can be asked." (D9, D20)
2. A separate branch "Important, not asked yet" lists never-asked topics the AI marked as
   fundamental, labelled "AI-suggested", with "disagree?". (D20, D23)
3. Opens "Laplace transforms" → real past questions, each beside its scan snippet. (D21)
4. Marks some done or hard; marks are private and synced. (A15)

### F-4 Misread
1. Rohan sees "x² + 4 = 0" but the scan beside it shows "x² − 4 = 0". Taps "Report a
   misread".
2. At 2 reports the question is hidden until someone corrects the text. (D21)

### F-5 Exam week
1. Rohan taps "Plan my 3 days" → 9 sessions of about 90 minutes, topics ordered by weighted
   frequency and moved up by his "hard" marks, each with 3 to 6 real past questions. (D19,
   A28)
2. Fewer than 3 papers for the course: every session reads "based on N papers: a rough
   guide". (D14, A13)
3. "Share plan" makes a link anyone can open without an account. (D11)

### F-6 Arjun deletes his account
Personal data is erased (name, email, marks, plans). His uploads stay as "uploaded by a
former student". (D17, A25)
