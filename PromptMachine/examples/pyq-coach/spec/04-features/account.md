# Account

**Tier:** v0 · **Sources:** D17, D18, D28, A3, A9, A24, A25

## Behaviour
1. Sign in with an @iitb.ac.in email login link; confirm 18 or over to create an account.
   Under-18s can use share links without an account.
2. If the link is opened inside WhatsApp's or Instagram's browser, show "Open in Chrome /
   Safari" first.
3. One account on every device; everything syncs.
4. Delete account in the app and from a web page; personal data erased within 90 days at
   most (target: immediately); uploads stay anonymous.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Given 50 sign-ups in an hour, then all receive login emails.
- [ ] Given account deletion, then name, email, marks and plans are gone and uploads show
      "uploaded by a former student".
