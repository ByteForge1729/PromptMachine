# Scope

## v0: one observing season, one club

| Feature | Why it is in v0 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Membership by invite link | Members-only from day one | D3, D7, A8, A9 |
| Quick log, works offline | The logbook is the core; dark sites have no signal | D2, D5, A4, A11, A12 |
| Logbook with notes added later | Logs are the club's memory | D2, A13 |
| Tonight on campus: hourly forecast + ranking | "When is it worth going out" | D1, D6, D9, A14 |
| Trip weekends: ranking across dark sites | Both jobs from day one | D1, D8, A10, A14 |
| Season view in cloudy months | Keeps the app useful June to September | D10, A17 |
| CSV export | Club memory must be portable | A19 |

Build order inside v0: membership, then logging, then tonight, then trips, then season
view. (A0)

## Non-goals

- **A star map or planetarium.** Stellarium already does it well. (A18)
- **Astrophotography processing.** A different tool. (A18)
- **A public showcase or social feed.** Members only. (D3)
- **Trip sign-ups, headcounts or cost splitting.** WhatsApp and SplitEasy do this. (D8)
- **A native app.** A website is enough for one club. (A18)
- **Promising clear skies.** (D9)

## Success

By the end of February: at least one club trip whose date was one of the app's top two
ranked weekends that week, with trip logs rated 4 or 5. (D11) Secondary, so that one
cloudy night does not decide the verdict: at least 15 members have logged 5 or more
nights. (A16)
