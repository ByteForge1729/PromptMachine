# Brief

## Original idea, verbatim

"A web app where our astronomy club logs what we observed each night, and it tells
members when the sky will be worth going out for."

## Read-back, as corrected

A members-only website for one college astronomy club, used on phones outdoors at
night. It does two jobs from the first version: a shared logbook that works with no
signal, and a forecast that ranks upcoming nights on campus and upcoming weekends at
dark-sky sites, showing the raw numbers and how far the forecasts agree, without ever
promising a clear sky. Trips are organised on WhatsApp as they are today.

## Principles

1. **Never present a night as a sure thing.** Rank, show the numbers and the model
   agreement, and never use "best", "go" or "will be clear". (D9)
2. **A log written in the field is never lost or duplicated.** (A4)
3. **Usable in the dark with cold hands:** red screen, three taps, no typing beyond two
   letters. (D2, A7)
4. **Members only.** Nothing is visible outside the club. (D3)
5. **The club's memory belongs to the club:** logs can always be exported. (A19)
